Definitions

Definition 1 Kernel and image

For a linear map T: V \to W the kernel is \ker T = \{\, v \in V : Tv = 0 \,\} and the image is \im T = \{\, Tv : v \in V \,\}. Both are subspaces.1

Two theorems

Theorem 1 Rank–nullity

If V is finite-dimensional then

\dim V = \dim \ker T + \dim \im T.(1)
Proof

Pick a basis … they are independent: if

\sum_{j=1}^{r} a_j\, Tv_j = 0

then \sum_j a_j v_j \in \ker T, so every a_j = 0. Counting gives (1).
