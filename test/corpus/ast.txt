# AST shapes and positions

One case per node type of the AST (`docs/api.html#node-types`), Tern's additions included, asserting the
type, its key fields and exact positions (`json ast`, before transforms):
lines and columns 1-based, columns and offsets in UTF-16 code units of the
normalised source, ends exclusive.

Extents follow mdast-util-from-markdown and its extensions
wherever an equivalent exists: the root ends at the end of the source; a
`break` includes its line ending; a container's label paragraph spans the
brackets; a task item's paragraph starts after `[x] `. Beyond mdast: a node's
position includes its glued attribute group; a `tableCell` spans its trimmed
content; a `cell` runs from its `+++` line (or, for the first cell, its first
content line) to its last content line. Also fixed: a headerless table has
`data.tern.headerless: true`; inline `html` nodes have `data.tern.kind:
"inline"`; adjacent text and soft breaks merge into one `text` node; the
parser attaches attribute lines, so table attributes are on the parse AST.

## ast/root

spec: syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#columns

The root spans the whole note, to the end of the source after the final line ending (here 2:1), as in mdast; one paragraph with one text node.

````tern
Hello.
````

````json ast
{"type":"root",
 "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":2,"column":1,"offset":7}},
 "children":[{"type":"paragraph",
   "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":1,"column":7,"offset":6}},
   "children":[{"type":"text","value":"Hello.",
     "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":1,"column":7,"offset":6}}}]}]}
````

## ast/paragraph-soft-break

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-inline#soft-break syntax-blocks#columns

A two-line paragraph: the soft break is a `\n` inside a text node whose position crosses the line, and the second line's stripped indentation is inside that span.

````tern
Let *x* be
  linear, `y` too.
````

````json ast
{"children":[{"type":"paragraph",
  "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":2,"column":19,"offset":29}},
  "children":[
   {"type":"text","value":"Let ",
    "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":1,"column":5,"offset":4}}},
   {"type":"emphasis",
    "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":5,"offset":4},"end":{"line":1,"column":8,"offset":7}},
    "children":[{"type":"text","value":"x",
      "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":6,"offset":5},"end":{"line":1,"column":7,"offset":6}}}]},
   {"type":"text","value":" be\nlinear, ",
    "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":8,"offset":7},"end":{"line":2,"column":11,"offset":21}}},
   {"type":"inlineCode","value":"y",
    "position":{"start":{"line":2,"column":11,"offset":21},"end":{"line":2,"column":14,"offset":24}}},
   {"type":"text","value":" too.",
    "position":{"start":{"line":2,"column":14,"offset":24},"end":{"line":2,"column":19,"offset":29}}}]}]}
````

## ast/table

spec: syntax-blocks#tables

A table records `align`; the delimiter row is not a node; rows span their lines and cells hold inline content. A `tableCell` spans its trimmed content, without the pipes and padding that mdast-util-gfm-table includes.

````tern
| Key | Action |
|:--|--:|
| `:w` | write |
````

````json ast
{"children":[{"type":"table","align":["left","right"],
  "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":3,"column":17,"offset":43}},
  "children":[
   {"type":"tableRow",
    "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":1,"offset":0},"end":{"line":1,"column":17,"offset":16}},
    "children":[
     {"type":"tableCell",
      "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":3,"offset":2},"end":{"line":1,"column":6,"offset":5}},
      "children":[{"type":"text","value":"Key",
        "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":3,"offset":2},"end":{"line":1,"column":6,"offset":5}}}]},
     {"type":"tableCell",
      "position":{"start":{"line":1,"column":9,"offset":8},"end":{"line":1,"column":15,"offset":14}},
      "children":[{"type":"text","value":"Action"}]}]},
   {"type":"tableRow",
    "position":{"start":{"line":3,"column":1,"offset":27},"end":{"line":3,"column":17,"offset":43}},
    "children":[
     {"type":"tableCell",
      "position":{"start":{"line":3,"column":3,"offset":29},"end":{"line":3,"column":7,"offset":33}},
      "children":[{"type":"inlineCode","value":":w",
        "position":{"start":{"line":3,"column":3,"offset":29},"end":{"line":3,"column":7,"offset":33}}}]},
     {"type":"tableCell",
      "position":{"start":{"line":3,"column":10,"offset":36},"end":{"line":3,"column":15,"offset":41}},
      "children":[{"type":"text","value":"write"}]}]}]}]}
````

## ast/table-attributes

spec: syntax-blocks#tables syntax-blocks#attribute-line
