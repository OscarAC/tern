# Headings

ATX headings on one line, the trailing strong attribute group, and slugs.
Every heading gets an id.

## heading/spec-example

spec: syntax-blocks#heading

The heading example: a trailing strong group gives the id and class.

````tern
## Kernel and image {#kernel .wide}
````

````html
<h2 id="kernel" class="wide">Kernel and image</h2>
````

## heading/levels

spec: syntax-blocks#heading

One to six `#` give `h1` to `h6`.

````tern
# One
## Two
### Three
#### Four
##### Five
###### Six
````

````html
<h1 id="one">One</h1>
<h2 id="two">Two</h2>
<h3 id="three">Three</h3>
<h4 id="four">Four</h4>
<h5 id="five">Five</h5>
<h6 id="six">Six</h6>
````

## heading/seven-hashes-is-text

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Seven `#` is not a heading.

````tern
####### Seven
````

````html
<p>####### Seven</p>
````

## heading/not-headings

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-inline#ambiguity

`#hashtag`, `#1 priority` and `C#` are not headings: a heading needs a space (or the end of the line) after the `#` run at the line start.

````tern
#hashtag
#1 priority
C# is a language
````

````html
<p>#hashtag
#1 priority
C# is a language</p>
````

## heading/tab-after-hashes

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-blocks#line-algorithm
options: escaped

A tab after the `#` run counts as a space (as in CommonMark), so `#<TAB>Title` is a heading.

````tern
#\tTitle
````

````html
<h1 id="title">Title</h1>
````


## heading/closing-hashes-kept

spec: syntax-blocks#heading

A closing run of `#` is text: `# C# #` keeps both, and so does `## Title ##`.

````tern
# C# #
## Title ##
````

````html
<h1 id="c">C# #</h1>
<h2 id="title">Title ##</h2>
````

## heading/slug-unicode

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs syntax-blocks#character-classes
options: schema=none

A slug keeps letters from any script; NFKC then lower case.

````tern
## Überblick
### 日本語
````

````html
<h2 id="überblick">Überblick</h2>
<h3 id="日本語">日本語</h3>
````

````diagnostics
````

## heading/slug-leading-digits

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs

Everything before the first letter or `_` is dropped, so section numbers do not reach the slug (and slugs stay referenceable).

````tern
## 2. Results
## 3.1 Paragraph
## 2nd edition
````

````html
<h2 id="results">2. Results</h2>
<h2 id="paragraph">3.1 Paragraph</h2>
<h2 id="nd-edition">2nd edition</h2>
````

## heading/slug-nfkc

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs syntax-blocks#character-classes

NFKC is applied before lower-casing: the ligature `ﬁ` and full-width letters fold to ASCII, and `²` becomes a digit that is then dropped as leading.

````tern
## ﬁle Ｓｙｓｔｅｍ
## ²nd place
````

````html
<h2 id="file-system">ﬁle Ｓｙｓｔｅｍ</h2>
<h2 id="nd-place">²nd place</h2>
````

## heading/slug-underscore-start

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs

A slug may start at `_`, and `_` is kept.

````tern
## _private
## __init__
````

````html
<h2 id="_private">_private</h2>
<h2 id="__init__">__init__</h2>
````

## heading/slug-no-letters

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs

With no letter or `_`, nothing is left and the slug is `section`; repeats get `-2`, `-3`, `-4`. An empty heading is allowed.

````tern
## 1984
## ---
## ①
##
````

````html
<h2 id="section">1984</h2>
<h2 id="section-2">---</h2>
<h2 id="section-3">①</h2>
<h2 id="section-4"></h2>
````

## heading/slug-combining-marks

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs syntax-blocks#character-classes

Combining marks (`\p{M}`) are kept by step 3, so Devanagari vowel signs and the virama stay in the slug, and `İ` lower-cases to `i` + U+0307 (Unicode default case mapping), which is kept too.

````tern
## हिन्दी
## İstanbul
````

````html
<h2 id="हिन्दी">हिन्दी</h2>
<h2 id="i̇stanbul">İstanbul</h2>
````

## heading/slug-duplicates

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs elements#ids

A repeated slug gets `-2`, then `-3`.

````tern
## Notes
## Notes
## Notes
````

````html
<h2 id="notes">Notes</h2>
<h2 id="notes-2">Notes</h2>
<h2 id="notes-3">Notes</h2>
````

## heading/slug-after-explicit-id

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs elements#ids

Slugs share one registry with explicit ids, and explicit ids are registered first: a slug that meets an id already taken gets `-2`, with no diagnostic.

````tern
## Intro {#notes}
## Notes
````

````html
<h2 id="notes">Intro</h2>
<h2 id="notes-2">Notes</h2>
````

## heading/explicit-id-after-slug-wins

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs elements#ids elements#references

Explicit ids beat slugs: ids an author wrote are registered before any slug, so an explicit `#kernel` that comes after a heading slugged `kernel` keeps its id, the earlier heading's slug yields to `kernel-2`, there is no diagnostic, and `@kernel` reaches the explicit id.

````tern
## Kernel
## Definition {#kernel}

See @kernel.
````

````html
<h2 id="kernel-2">Kernel</h2>
<h2 id="kernel">Definition</h2>
<p>See <a class="t-ref" href="#kernel">Definition</a>.</p>
````

````diagnostics
````

## heading/slug-from-text

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs

The slug is built from the plain text of the rendered inline content, so a link contributes its text and not its URL (from the source the id would be `see-the-docs-https-x-org`).

````tern
## See [the docs](https://x.org)
````

````html
<h2 id="see-the-docs">See <a href="https://x.org">the docs</a></h2>
````

## heading/slug-plain-text

spec: syntax-blocks#slugs syntax-inline#raw-inline-html syntax-inline#entities

The plain text the slug is built from: tags dropped, entities decoded, math as its TeX, footnote references ignored. From the source the id would be `caf-eacute-b-bold-b-t-n`; with the footnote number it would end in `-1`.

````tern
## Caf&eacute; <b>bold</b> $T^*$[^n]

[^n]: A note.
````

````html
<h2 id="café-bold-t">Caf&eacute; <b>bold</b> <span class="t-math">T^*</span><sup class="t-fnref"><a id="fnref-n" href="#fn-n">1</a></sup></h2>
<section class="t-footnotes"><ol><li id="fn-n"><p>A note. <a class="t-fnback" href="#fnref-n">↩</a></p></li></ol></section>
````

````diagnostics
````

## heading/inline-content

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-inline#algorithm

Heading text is inline content.

````tern
## *Emph* and `code`
````

````html
<h2 id="emph-and-code"><em>Emph</em> and <code>code</code></h2>
````

## heading/attrs-strong-only

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#strong-and-weak syntax-blocks#positions

Only a strong group binds to a heading; weak (`{x}`, `{open}`) and unparsable (`{1, 2}`) groups stay heading text, silently.

````tern
## The set {x}
## Options {open}
## Pairs {1, 2}
## Kernel {.wide}
````

````html
<h2 id="the-set-x">The set {x}</h2>
<h2 id="options-open">Options {open}</h2>
<h2 id="pairs-1-2">Pairs {1, 2}</h2>
<h2 id="kernel" class="wide">Kernel</h2>
````

## heading/attrs-glued-or-spaced

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#positions

The group may be glued to the text or follow spaces; an explicit id is used as written.

````tern
## Kernel{#Ker_nel}
## Image   {#img}
````

````html
<h2 id="Ker_nel">Kernel</h2>
<h2 id="img">Image</h2>
````

## heading/attrs-key-value

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#merging-and-emission

A group with `key=value` is strong; without `#id` the heading still gets its slug.

````tern
## Title {data-x=1 .a}
````

````html
<h2 id="title" class="a" data-x="1">Title</h2>
````

## heading/attrs-malformed

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#positions

A trailing group starting with `#` that does not parse stays heading text, with `attr.malformed` at the first bad column, which is the first character the grammar rejects: the `!`, column 14, not the `#` of the item or the `{`.

````tern
## Kernel {#k!}
````

````html
<h2 id="kernel-k">Kernel {#k!}</h2>
````

````diagnostics
1:14 warning attr.malformed
````

## heading/attrs-malformed-once

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#positions

A trailing group that does not parse gets `attr.malformed` once, at the first bad column, also when it is glued to a `]` or a `*` closer, where the inline parser sees a failed group too.

````tern
## Title [x]{#a!}
## *T*{#b!}
````

````html
<h2 id="title-x-a">Title [x]{#a!}</h2>
<h2 id="t-b"><em>T</em>{#b!}</h2>
````

````diagnostics
1:16 warning attr.malformed
2:10 warning attr.malformed
````

## heading/group-not-at-end-is-text

spec: syntax-blocks#heading-attributes syntax-blocks#positions

A group that is not at the end of the line is text, and is not a malformed trailing group.

````tern
## {#a} title
````

````html
<h2 id="a-title">{#a} title</h2>
````

## heading/escaped-brace-not-group

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-inline#escapes

`\{` makes the trailing group text, with no diagnostic.

````tern
## Title \{#t}
````

````html
<h2 id="title-t">Title {#t}</h2>
````

## heading/no-continuation

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-blocks#r1

A heading ends on its line; nothing continues it.

````tern
# Title
continued
````

````html
<h1 id="title">Title</h1>
<p>continued</p>
````

## heading/no-setext

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-blocks#r1 syntax-blocks#thematic-break

There are no setext headings: a block is recognised from its first line, so `=====` is paragraph text and `---` is a rule.

````tern
Title
=====

Other
---
````

````html
<p>Title
=====</p>
<p>Other</p>
<hr>
````

## heading/interrupts-paragraph

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Row 5: a heading interrupts a paragraph.

````tern
Some prose
## Next
````

````html
<p>Some prose</p>
<h2 id="next">Next</h2>
````

## heading/trailing-backslash-literal

spec: syntax-blocks#heading syntax-inline#hard-break

A `\` at the end of a heading is literal: a heading is a one-line construct.

````tern
## Title\
````

````html
<h2 id="title">Title\</h2>
````
