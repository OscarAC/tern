# Numbering and references

Numbering and references: counters shared by name, document
order, equations numbered only with an id, labels, the default reference
text, the `ref=` selector and validated fragment links. The fixture schema puts
theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary on the counter `theorem` with
`ref: '{label} {n}'`; definition, example and exercise have their own
counters and no `ref` template.

Also: a labelled target that is neither counted nor titled reads as
its label; a `ref=` value outside `label|number|title|full` gets
`attr.malformed` and the default text; a part the target lacks gives the
default text silently; a fragment link to a missing id gets `ref.dangling` at
its `[` and stays a link. The fixture's `problem` counts `within: 'h2'`:
the `numbering/within-*` cases pin numbering per section, with the extended
schema's `claim` (within h3), `axiom` (within h1) and `remark` (on
`problem`'s counter, with no `within` of its own).

## numbering/shared-counter

spec: elements#numbering

Names that share a counter share one sequence; each keeps its own label.

````tern
:::theorem
A.
:::

:::lemma
B.
:::

:::theorem
C.
:::
````

````html
<section class="t-block theorem" data-t="theorem"><div class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Theorem 1</span></div><p>A.</p></section>
<section class="t-block lemma" data-t="lemma"><div class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Lemma 2</span></div><p>B.</p></section>
<section class="t-block theorem" data-t="theorem"><div class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Theorem 3</span></div><p>C.</p></section>
````

## numbering/document-order-nested

spec: elements#numbering

Numbers follow document order: an outer block is numbered at its opener, before the blocks nested in it.

````tern
:::theorem
:::lemma

:::
Outer.
:::

:::corollary
After.
:::
````

````html

````

## numbering/ref-unresolved-literal

spec: elements#references syntax-inline#reference syntax-blocks#r7

An unresolved `@name` stays literal text, with `ref.dangling` at the `@` and a hint to escape it.

````tern
Ask @alice.
````

````html
<p>Ask @alice.</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:5 warning ref.dangling "\@alice"
````
