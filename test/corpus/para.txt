# Paragraphs

What starts, continues and ends a paragraph, and how its lines are joined.
The per-row interrupt cases are in block.txt.

## para/spec-example

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph

The paragraph example: consecutive lines that start nothing else are one paragraph, joined by a soft break.

````tern
Let $T: V \to W$ be linear.
Its kernel is a subspace.
````

````html
<p>Let <span class="t-math">T: V \to W</span> be linear.
Its kernel is a subspace.</p>
````

## para/blank-lines-separate

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Any number of blank lines ends a paragraph and produces nothing.

````tern
One



Two
````

````html
<p>One</p>
<p>Two</p>
````

## para/lines-stripped

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#r2
options: escaped

Each line's leading and trailing whitespace is stripped before the lines are joined with `\n`.

````tern
  first
\t\tsecond\t
````

````html
<p>first
second</p>
````

````json ast
{"type":"root","children":[{"type":"paragraph","children":[{"type":"text","value":"first\nsecond"}]}]}
````

## para/offset-table-positions

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#columns

The offset table maps joined text back to the source, so an inline diagnostic on an indented continuation line lands on its real column and offset.

````tern
First line
    then press:kbd[S] here
````

````html
<p>First line
then press:kbd[S] here</p>
````

````json diagnostics
[{"code":"inline.glued","severity":"info","position":{"start":{"line":2,"column":15,"offset":25}}}]
````

## para/ordered-non-1-does-not-interrupt

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#lists syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Only an ordered item numbered 1 interrupts a paragraph, so a wrapped line starting `2019.` stays prose.

````tern
The archive covers everything up to
2019. was a year of change.
````

````html
<p>The archive covers everything up to
2019. was a year of change.</p>
````

## para/ordered-01-interrupts

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#lists syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

The interrupt rule looks at the value, not the spelling (as in CommonMark): `01.` is an ordered item numbered 1, so it interrupts the paragraph, and the list gets no `start`.

````tern
Some prose
01. first
````

````html
<p>Some prose</p>
<ol><li>first</li></ol>
````

## para/empty-items-do-not-interrupt

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#lists syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

An empty bullet or an empty `1.` does not interrupt a paragraph; both lines continue it.

````tern
Some prose
-
1.
````

````html
<p>Some prose
-
1.</p>
````

## para/non-block-starts-continue

spec: syntax-blocks#paragraph syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-inline#ambiguity

Lines that look like block starts but are not (no space after the marker) continue the paragraph.

````tern
Some prose
#hashtag
>5 people
-dash
````

````html
<p>Some prose
#hashtag
&gt;5 people
-dash</p>
````
