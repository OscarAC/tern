# Inline math

Inline math, verbatim math and `$$` in the middle of a line, with the `$`
rows of the ambiguity rules and the dollar residuals. First every row of the inline-math outcome table, then the
opener and closer clauses one at a time, the diagnostics with their negatives, atomicity,
glued groups, and verbatim math.

Positions: `math.inline-display`, `math.unclosed-inline` and `math.bracket` are at "the `$`"
(the opener, or the first `$` of a `$$` run; `math.bracket` is always at the opener);
`math.suspect-price` is at the opener. `math.inline-display` fires once per `$$` run.

## inline-math/spelling

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

The inline-math spelling example: the content is raw TeX, so `_`, `^` and `*` inside it are not Tern.

````tern
$\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^*$
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^*</span></p>
````

## inline-math/price-then-price

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#ambiguity

Outcome table: `costs $5 and $10` is text, because the candidate closer is preceded by a space, and the second `$` has no closer.

````tern
costs $5 and $10
````

````html
<p>costs $5 and $10</p>
````

## inline-math/price-then-math

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

Outcome table: in `costs $5 and $x$` the first `$` fails on the candidate before `x`, which then gets its own chance and opens.

````tern
costs $5 and $x$
````

````html
<p>costs $5 and <span class="t-math">x</span></p>
````

## inline-math/paren-dollar-usd

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

Outcome table: in `$100 ($USD) and $x$` the bracket clause rejects the candidate after `(`, `$USD` fails on the whitespace before the next dollar, and `$x$` is math.

````tern
$100 ($USD) and $x$
````

````html
<p>$100 ($USD) and <span class="t-math">x</span></p>
````

## inline-math/paren-dollar-pair

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

````tern
Cost ($) and revenue ($)
````

````html
<p>Cost ($) and revenue ($)</p>
````

## inline-math/table-paren-dollar

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-blocks#tables

````tern

|---|---|
| 5 | 10 |
````

````html

</table>
````

## inline-math/closer-after-escaped-bracket

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#escapes

The closer clause rejects only an unescaped `(`, `[` or `{`; after `\{`, `\[` or `\(` the dollar closes.



## inline-math/first-dollar-decides

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

The first dollar after the opener is the only candidate: it fails here (space before it), and the later `$` that could have closed is never considered.

````tern
$x $ y$
````

````html
<p>$x $ y$</p>
````

## inline-math/candidate-reopens

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math

A rejected candidate gets its own chance to open: `$a ` is text and `$b$` is math.

````tern
$a $b$
````

````html
<p>$a <span class="t-math">b</span></p>
````

## inline-math/escaped-dollar-outside

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#escapes syntax-inline#ambiguity

Outside math `\$` is a literal dollar and never opens or closes.

````tern

````

````html
<p>costs $5 and $10</p>
````

## inline-math/escaped-opener

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#ambiguity syntax-inline#residual-dollars

`\$` is the fix for a price misread as math: the escaped dollar is not a dollar, and the last `$` has nothing after it.

````tern

````


## inline-math/escaped-closer

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#escape

`$x\$` is text: the closer search skips `\$`, nothing closes, and the opener being text, `\$` is then an ordinary escape.

````tern
$x\$
````

````html
<p>$x$</p>
````

## inline-math/backslash-pair-skipping

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#escapes

The closer search skips `\x` pairs from the left, so in `a\\$` the `\\` is a pair and the following dollar closes.

````tern
$a\\$ and $b$
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">a\\</span> and <span class="t-math">b</span></p>
````

## inline-math/escaped-dollar-not-adjacent

spec: syntax-inline#inline-math syntax-inline#escape

An escaped `$` is not a dollar, so the `$` after `\$` is not adjacent to one and may open.

````tern
\$$x$
````


## inline-math/tex-marks-outside-math

spec: syntax-blocks#r4 syntax-inline#entities

TeX's marks outside math are plain text: `^`, `_`, an unbraced-position `{…}`, and `&` (escaped, not an entity).

````tern

````


````tern

````

````html
<p><span id="e" class="t-math">x</span></p>
````


## inline-math/inline-display-then-math

spec: syntax-inline#inline-display syntax-inline#inline-math

A mid-line `$$` does not disturb inline math later in the paragraph.

````tern
a $$ b $x$
````

````html
<p>a $$ b <span class="t-math">x</span></p>
````

````diagnostics
1:3 info math.inline-display
````

## inline-math/verbatim-form

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math syntax-inline#residual-dollars

Verbatim math `` $`…` `` is always math, with no price or bracket rules: the escape hatch for `\$` and for `]0,1[`.

````tern
Write $`\$5 + \$10` or $`]0,1[`.
````

````html
<p>Write <span class="t-math">\$5 + \$10</span> or <span class="t-math">]0,1[</span>.</p>
````

## inline-math/verbatim-double-backticks

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math

A run of n backticks after `$` closes at the next run of exactly n, so a double run can hold a single backtick.

````tern
$``a`b``
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">a`b</span></p>
````

## inline-math/verbatim-atom

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math syntax-inline#algorithm

Verbatim math is an atom: no emphasis and no reference inside it.

````tern
$`*a* @b`
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">*a* @b</span></p>
````

## inline-math/verbatim-no-closing-run

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math syntax-inline#ambiguity

With no closing run the `$` is text and the backtick is processed normally (a lone backtick is literal).

````tern
cost $`5
````

````html
<p>cost $`5</p>
````

## inline-math/verbatim-unclosed-then-dollar

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math syntax-inline#algorithm

A failed verbatim opener followed later by a dollar.

The tokenizer's rule 3 defers to verbatim math.

````tern
$`x$
````

````html
<p>$`x$</p>
````

## inline-math/verbatim-escaped-dollar

spec: syntax-inline#verbatim-math syntax-inline#escape

`` \$` `` is a literal dollar followed by an ordinary code span.

````tern
\$`x`
````

````html
<p>$<code>x</code></p>
````

## inline-math/code-span-first

spec: syntax-inline#algorithm syntax-inline#code-span syntax-inline#inline-math

Left to right, the first atom wins: a code span that starts first protects the dollar inside it.

````tern
`a$` and b$
````

````html
<p><code>a$</code> and b$</p>
````

## inline-math/math-first

spec: syntax-inline#algorithm syntax-inline#inline-math

Left to right, math that opens first takes a backtick into its TeX; the candidate closer search sees only dollars and `\x` pairs.

````tern
$a `b$ c`
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">a `b</span> c`</p>
````
