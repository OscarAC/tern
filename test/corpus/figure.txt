# Figures

Figures: the explicit `:::figure`, the implicit figure (an
image alone in a paragraph carrying `#id`, or with `#id` on the attribute
line before it), the alt rule and the lone image without `<p>`. Both
the no-`<p>` rule and "not inside a figure" go by the tag,
and "inside" means any ancestor. The fixture schema's `figure` is `{tag: 'figure',
counter: 'figure', label: 'Figure'}`.

## figure/explicit-body-caption-last

spec: elements#figures elements#title-slots

An explicit figure takes any body; its caption is the last child, with the counted label first.

````tern
:::figure[Two ospreys]{#fig-pair}
Ospreys at dawn.
:::
````

````html
<figure id="fig-pair" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><p>Ospreys at dawn.</p><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Two ospreys</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/explicit-lone-image-no-p

spec: elements#figures

A figure whose body is a single image-only paragraph emits the `<img>` without a `<p>`; the image's alt is its own bracket text.

````tern
:::figure[An osprey]{#fig-o}
![Osprey](osprey.jpg)
:::
````

````html
<figure id="fig-o" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt="Osprey"><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> An osprey</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/explicit-two-images-keep-p

spec: elements#figures

Only a single image-only paragraph loses its `<p>`; a body of two image paragraphs keeps both.

````tern
:::figure[Before and after]
![Before](a.jpg)

![After](b.jpg)
:::
````

````html
<figure class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><p><img src="a.jpg" alt="Before"></p><p><img src="b.jpg" alt="After"></p><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Before and after</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/explicit-not-doubled

spec: elements#figures

An image with `#id` inside an explicit figure is not an implicit figure: one figure, one number, and the image keeps its id and alt.

````tern
:::figure[Two ospreys]{#fig-pair}
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#img-osprey}
:::
````

````html
<figure id="fig-pair" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img id="img-osprey" src="osprey.jpg" alt="Osprey"><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Two ospreys</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/explicit-without-title

spec: elements#figures elements#title-slots

A figure without a written title still gets a caption holding its label.

````tern
:::figure{#fig-x}
![A chart](chart.png)
:::
````

````html
<figure id="fig-x" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="chart.png" alt="A chart"><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span></figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-example

spec: elements#figures

The implicit-figure example: the bracket text is the caption, `#id` goes to the figure, `alt=` and the other attributes stay on `<img>`.

````tern
![Osprey, 06:40.](osprey.jpg){#fig-osprey width=70% alt="An osprey in flight"}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-osprey" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt="An osprey in flight" width="70%"><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Osprey, 06:40.</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-alt-empty

spec: elements#figures

Without `alt=`, an implicit figure's image has an empty alt, so the caption is read once.

````tern
![Osprey, 06:40.](osprey.jpg){#fig-osprey}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-osprey" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Osprey, 06:40.</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-needs-id

spec: elements#figures syntax-inline#links

An image without an id is always just an image in its paragraph, with or without other attributes.

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg)

![Osprey](osprey.jpg){.wide}
````

````html
<p><img src="osprey.jpg" alt="Osprey"></p>
<p><img class="wide" src="osprey.jpg" alt="Osprey"></p>
````

## figure/implicit-caption-inline

spec: elements#figures

The caption is the bracket text as inline content, markup and math included.

````tern
![The *kernel* of $T$](kernel.svg){#fig-ker}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-ker" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="kernel.svg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> The <em>kernel</em> of <span class="t-math">T</span></figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-image-attrs-stay

spec: elements#figures

Only the `#id` moves to the figure; the image's classes and other attributes stay on `<img>`.

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#fig-o .round width=50%}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-o" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img class="round" src="osprey.jpg" alt="" width="50%"><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Osprey</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-attribute-line

spec: elements#figures syntax-blocks#attribute-line

An attribute line before the paragraph goes to the figure, after `t-block figure`.

````tern
{.wide}
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#fig-o}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-o" class="t-block figure wide" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Osprey</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-not-with-text

spec: elements#figures

A paragraph holding an image and text is not a figure, even when the image has an id.

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#img-o} at dawn.
````

````html
<p><img id="img-o" src="osprey.jpg" alt="Osprey"> at dawn.</p>
````

## figure/implicit-not-two-images

spec: elements#figures

A paragraph of two images is not a figure: the only content must be one image.

````tern
![A](a.jpg){#img-a} ![B](b.jpg){#img-b}
````

````html
<p><img id="img-a" src="a.jpg" alt="A"> <img id="img-b" src="b.jpg" alt="B"></p>
````

## figure/implicit-not-inside-figure-tag

spec: elements#figures elements#names

The "not inside a figure" condition is about the tag: inside `:::gallery{tag=figure}` an image with `#id` stays an image.

````tern
:::gallery{tag=figure}
![A](a.jpg){#img-a}

![B](b.jpg)
:::
````

````html
<figure class="t-block gallery" data-t="gallery"><p><img id="img-a" src="a.jpg" alt="A"></p><p><img src="b.jpg" alt="B"></p></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-inside-other-container

spec: elements#figures

Inside an element whose tag is not `figure`, the implicit figure applies as usual.

````tern
:::box
![Nest](nest.jpg){#fig-nest}
:::
````

````html
<div class="t-block box" data-t="box"><figure id="fig-nest" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="nest.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Nest</figcaption></figure></div>
````

## figure/implicit-without-schema

spec: elements#figures elements#allowlists
options: schema=none

The implicit figure is a core transform: without a schema it is still a `figure` (from the allowlist), with no label or number.

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#fig-o}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-o" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title">Osprey</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-empty-caption

spec: elements#figures elements#title-slots

Empty brackets give a caption holding only the label.

````tern
![](a.jpg){#fig-a}
````

````html
<figure id="fig-a" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="a.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span></figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/attribute-line-id-makes-figure

spec: elements#figures syntax-blocks#attribute-line

An attribute line with `#id` before an image-only paragraph makes the implicit figure, as if the image carried the id. The bracket text is the caption and the alt is empty.

````tern
{#fig-a}
![A](a.jpg)
````

````html
<figure id="fig-a" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="a.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> A</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/attribute-line-class-only-no-figure

spec: elements#figures syntax-blocks#attribute-line

Without an id anywhere, an attribute line on an image-only paragraph does not make a figure; its attributes go to the `<p>`.

````tern
{.wide}
![A](a.jpg)
````

````html
<p class="wide"><img src="a.jpg" alt="A"></p>
````

## figure/no-p-by-tag

spec: elements#figures elements#names

The no-`<p>` rule follows the tag, not the name: a `:::gallery{tag=figure}` whose body is one image-only paragraph emits the bare `<img>`.

````tern
:::gallery{tag=figure}
![A](a.jpg)
:::
````

````html
<figure class="t-block gallery" data-t="gallery"><img src="a.jpg" alt="A"></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-not-inside-figure-ancestor

spec: elements#figures

"Not inside a figure" means any ancestor: an image with `#id` in a box inside a figure stays an image, and the box keeps its `<p>` because its own tag is not `figure`.

````tern
:::figure[Pair]{#fig-p}
:::box
![A](a.jpg){#img-a}
:::
:::
````

````html
<figure id="fig-p" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><div class="t-block box" data-t="box"><p><img id="img-a" src="a.jpg" alt="A"></p></div><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Pair</figcaption></figure>
````

## figure/implicit-referenced

spec: elements#figures elements#references

An implicit figure is a counted block, so a reference to it reads "Figure 1".

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#fig-o}

See @fig-o.
````

````html
<figure id="fig-o" class="t-block figure" data-t="figure"><img src="osprey.jpg" alt=""><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Figure 1</span> Osprey</figcaption></figure>
<p>See <a class="t-ref" href="#fig-o">Figure 1</a>.</p>
````

## figure/implicit-same-node

spec: elements#figures elements#numbering

The implicit figure becomes the same node as `:::figure[bracket text]{#id}`: a `figure` containerDirective holding the id and the number.

````tern
![Osprey](osprey.jpg){#fig-o}
````

````json transformed
{"children":[{"type":"containerDirective","name":"figure","attributes":{"id":"fig-o"},
  "data":{"tern":{"counter":"figure","number":1,"text":"Figure 1"}}}]}
````

## figure/end-mark-before-caption

spec: elements#title-slots elements#output-contract

A figure's caption is its last child, so an end mark from the schema goes just before it, after the body.
In a figure the end mark comes just before the `figcaption`, which HTML allows only first or last.

````tern
:::proof[Sketch]{tag=figure}
Body.
:::
````

````html
<figure class="t-block proof" data-t="proof"><p>Body.</p><span class="t-end" aria-hidden="true">∎</span><figcaption class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Proof</span> Sketch</figcaption></figure>
````
