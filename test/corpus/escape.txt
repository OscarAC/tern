# Escapes and hard breaks

Cases for backslash escapes and hard breaks (prose escapes, no escapes inside
atoms), the `\` rows of the ambiguity rules, and the list residuals whose fix is an escape.
Line-start escapes live with their blocks.

## escape/all-ascii-punctuation

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#escapes

All 32 ASCII punctuation characters can be escaped, back to back.

````tern
\!\"\#\$\%\&\'\(\)\*\+\,\-\.\/\:\;\<\=\>\?\@\[\\\]\^\_\`\{\|\}\~
````

````html
<p>!"#$%&amp;'()*+,-./:;&lt;=&gt;?@[\]^_`{|}~</p>
````

## escape/backslash-before-other-literal

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#r4

A backslash before a digit, a space, a non-ASCII letter or non-ASCII punctuation is a literal backslash.

````tern
\1, a \ b, \é and \—
````

````html
<p>\1, a \ b, \é and \—</p>
````

## escape/hard-break

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-inline#ambiguity

`\` at the end of a paragraph line that is not the last is a hard break.

````tern
a\
b
````

````html
<p>a<br>
b</p>
````

## escape/hard-break-trailing-whitespace

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-blocks#r3

Lines are right-trimmed first, so spaces after the backslash do not stop the hard break.

````tern js
'a\\   \nb\n'
````

````html
<p>a<br>
b</p>
````

## escape/backslash-last-line-literal

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-inline#ambiguity

At the end of a block (the paragraph's last line) the backslash is literal.

````tern
a
b\
````

````html
<p>a
b\</p>
````

## escape/backslash-before-interrupting-heading

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-blocks#paragraph

A heading interrupts the paragraph, so the backslash ends the block and is literal.

````tern
a\
# H
````

````html
<p>a\</p>
<h1 id="h">H</h1>
````

## escape/backslash-end-of-heading-literal

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-blocks#heading syntax-inline#ambiguity

A heading is a one-line construct: a trailing backslash is literal (and the slug drops it).

````tern
## Title\
````

````html
<h2 id="title">Title\</h2>
````

## escape/hard-break-in-quote

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-blocks#blockquote

A hard break works in a paragraph inside any container.

````tern
> a\
> b
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a<br>
b</p></blockquote>
````

## escape/list-item-break-then-literal

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-blocks#lists

Inside a list item a mid-paragraph backslash breaks; the one at the end of the item's paragraph is literal.

````tern
- a\
  b\
- c
````

````html
<ul><li>a<br>
b\</li><li>c</li></ul>
````

## escape/hard-break-in-element

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-inline#inline-element

Element content may cross a line, and a trailing backslash there is a hard break inside the element.

````tern
:kbd[a\
b]
````

````html
<p><kbd class="kbd" data-t="kbd">a<br>
b</kbd></p>
````

## escape/escaped-backslash-then-break

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break syntax-inline#ambiguity

Left to right, `\\\` at the end of a line is an escaped backslash followed by a hard break. `latex.linebreak` fires only when a soft break follows the `\\`, that is, when a line break was actually lost; here a hard break follows, so it is silent.

````tern
a\\\
b
````

````html
<p>a\<br>
b</p>
````

````diagnostics
````

## escape/escaped-backslash-last-line

spec: syntax-inline#hard-break

`\\` at the end of the paragraph's last line is a literal backslash. No soft break follows it, so no line break was lost and `latex.linebreak` is silent.

````tern
a\\
````

````html
<p>a\</p>
````

````diagnostics
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-once

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters diagnostics#latex-delimiters

`\(` at the start of a line outside math is an escape (no math), with `latex.delimiters` (I) once per note: the later `\begin{` is silent.

````tern
\(a\) is not math.

\begin{align} x \end{align}
````

````html
<p>(a) is not math.</p>
<p>\begin{align} x \end{align}</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:1 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-bracket

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters

`\[` at the start of a line gets the hint too; the escaped brackets are text.

````tern
\[x^2\]
````

````html
<p>[x^2]</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:1 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-escapes-silent

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters

Escaped brackets around prose, and empty or escape-only delimiters, are escapes, not LaTeX: no hint.

````tern
See \[not a span\], \(\), \( \) and \[\*\].
````

````html
<p>See [not a span], (), ( ) and [*].</p>
````

````diagnostics
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-paren-text

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters

`\(…\)` with any content gets the hint; brackets need TeX inside.

````tern
\(see below\) and \[a\]
````

````html
<p>(see below) and [a]</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:1 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-mid-line

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters

Only `\begin{` must start a line; `\(` anywhere in a paragraph gets the hint when the matching `\)` follows it in the same paragraph. The escapes still apply.

````tern
see \(x\) here
````

````html
<p>see (x) here</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:5 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-in-heading

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters syntax-blocks#heading

`latex.delimiters` counts in any inline content, headings included; the slug is built from the escaped text.

````tern
## The map \(T\)
````

````html
<h2 id="the-map-t">The map (T)</h2>
````

````diagnostics
1:12 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-continuation-line

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters syntax-blocks#paragraph diagnostics#latex-delimiters

A paragraph's later line counts too; leading whitespace is stripped but the column is the backslash's own.

````tern
Consider
  \begin{cases} x \end{cases}
````

````html
<p>Consider
\begin{cases} x \end{cases}</p>
````

````diagnostics
2:3 info latex.delimiters
````

## escape/latex-delimiters-in-display-math-silent

spec: syntax-inline#latex-delimiters syntax-inline#escapes

Inside display math the backslash belongs to TeX: no hint.

````tern
$$
\begin{aligned} x \end{aligned}
$$
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>\begin{aligned} x \end{aligned}</span></div>
````

## escape/in-heading

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#heading

Escapes apply in headings.

````tern
## a\*b\* {#ab}
````

````html
<h2 id="ab">a*b*</h2>
````

## escape/in-container-title

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#container

`\]` escapes inside a container title.

````tern
:::box[a\]b]
x
:::
````

````html
<div class="t-block box" data-t="box"><div class="t-title">a]b</div><p>x</p></div>
````

## escape/in-element-content

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-inline#inline-element

`\]` inside an inline element is a literal bracket, not the closer.

````tern
:kbd[\]]
````

````html
<p><kbd class="kbd" data-t="kbd">]</kbd></p>
````

## escape/in-leaf-content

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#leaf

Escapes apply in leaf content.

````tern
::p[a\*b\*]
````

````html
<p class="p" data-t="p">a*b*</p>
````

## escape/in-table-cell

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#tables

Escapes apply in table cells.

````tern
| A |
|---|
| a\*b |
````

````html
<table><thead><tr><th>A</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>a*b</td></tr></tbody></table>
````

## escape/none-in-fence

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#fence

Fence content is verbatim: no escapes.

````tern
```
\*
```
````

````html
<pre data-t="code"><code>\*</code></pre>
````

## escape/none-in-attribute-values

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-blocks#finding-the-group

Attribute values take no escapes, quoted or not, although `\*` and `\.` are escapes in prose.

````tern
[x]{title="a\*b" data-k=\.}
````

````html
<p><span title="a\*b" data-k="\.">x</span></p>
````

## escape/none-in-raw-inline-html

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-inline#raw-inline-html

A raw inline tag is an atom: the backslash inside its attribute stays.

````tern
a <span title="\*">b</span>
````

````html
<p>a <span title="\*">b</span></p>
````

## escape/none-in-math

spec: syntax-inline#escapes syntax-inline#inline-math

Inside `$…$` the backslash belongs to TeX; the TeX reaches the output unchanged.

````tern
$\{x\}$
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math">\{x\}</span></p>
````

## escape/bracket-not-link

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#ambiguity syntax-inline#links

`\[` is the literal spelling of a bracket: no link forms.

````tern
\[a](u)
````

````html
<p>[a](u)</p>
````

## escape/colon-not-element

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#ambiguity syntax-inline#inline-element

`\:` is the literal spelling of an inline-element colon.

````tern
\:kbd[x]
````

````html
<p>:kbd[x]</p>
````

## escape/run-broken-by-escape

spec: syntax-inline#escape syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#ambiguity

An escaped `~` or `=` is not part of a delimiter run, so what remains are runs of one: text.

````tern
\~~a\~~ and \==b\==
````

````html
<p>~~a~~ and ==b==</p>
````

## escape/residual-year-list

spec: syntax-inline#residual-lists syntax-blocks#lists syntax-inline#escapes

After a blank line `2019. was a year` is an ordered list (CommonMark); `2019\.` is the fix.

````tern
Busy.

2019. was a year

2019\. was a year
````

````html
<p>Busy.</p>
<ol start="2019"><li>was a year</li></ol>
<p>2019. was a year</p>
````

## escape/residual-wrapped-bullet

spec: syntax-inline#residual-lists syntax-blocks#lists syntax-inline#escapes

A wrapped prose line starting `+ ` interrupts the paragraph as a bullet; `\+` keeps it prose.

````tern
The total is 5
+ 3 more.

The total is 5
\+ 3 more.
````

````html
<p>The total is 5</p>
<ul><li>3 more.</li></ul>
<p>The total is 5
+ 3 more.</p>
````
