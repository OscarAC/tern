# Display math

Display math: the three opener forms, the closer and its attributes, the terminators
(each ends the block with `math.unclosed` at the opener and is then reprocessed), numbering
and `@eq` references, and display math inside containers, quotes and list items. Inline math,
verbatim math and `$$` in the middle of a line are in inline-math.txt.

Conventions used throughout: display math is `<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math"
data-display>TeX</span></div>` (README, output conventions); TeX lines are joined with `\n`;
an unclosed block is `<div class="t-eq t-error">` holding its escaped source (the remainders,
without prefixes, inside a quote or list item). The whitespace of a TeX value: the
rest of the opener line loses its leading whitespace, TeX lines in an indented body lose up to
the opener's indentation, and the closer line's leading whitespace is not TeX.

## math/spec-example

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math elements#numbering

The display-math example: a multi-line equation with an id on its closer is numbered; the one-line form after it is not.

````tern
$$
\dim V = \dim \ker T + \dim \im T.
$$ {#eq-rn}

$$ e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 $$
````

````html
<div class="t-eq" id="eq-rn"><span class="t-math" data-display>\dim V = \dim \ker T + \dim \im T.</span><span class="t-eqno">(1)</span></div>
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0</span></div>
````

## math/multi-line

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math syntax-blocks#lines

Form (c) with an empty rest: the lines between the opener and the closer are the TeX, joined with `\n`.

````tern
$$
a = b
c = d
$$
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>a = b
c = d</span></div>
````

````json ast
{"type":"root","children":[{"type":"math","value":"a = b\nc = d"}]}
````

## math/non-terminator-lines

## math/blank-line-ends-with-error-heading-survives

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math diagnostics#three-mistakes

The third likely mistake: a blank line inside `$$` ends the block with `math.unclosed`; the intended closer then opens a second block that the next blank line ends too, and the heading survives. Each `math.unclosed` runs from its opener to where its terminating blank line starts.

````tern
$$
a = b

c = d
$$

## Next
````

````html
<div class="t-eq t-error">$$
a = b</div>
<p>c = d</p>
<div class="t-eq t-error">$$</div>
<h2 id="next">Next</h2>
````

````diagnostics
1:1-3:1 error math.unclosed "line 3"
5:1-6:1 error math.unclosed
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>c = d</span></div>
<div class="t-eq" id="eq-e"><span class="t-math" data-display>e = f</span><span class="t-eqno">(2)</span></div>
<p>See <a class="t-ref" href="#eq-a">(1)</a> and <a class="t-ref" href="#eq-e">(2)</a>.</p>
````

## math/ref-dangling

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math syntax-inline#reference elements#references

A reference to an equation id that does not exist stays literal, with `ref.dangling`.

````tern
$$ x = 1 $$

See @eq-x.
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>x = 1</span></div>
<p>See @eq-x.</p>
````

````diagnostics
3:5 warning ref.dangling
````

## math/attribute-line-id-numbers

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math syntax-blocks#attribute-line elements#numbering

An attribute line before display math attaches to `div.t-eq`; its id numbers the equation like a closer id.

````tern
{#eq-a}
$$
x = 1
$$

See @eq-a.
````

````html
<div class="t-eq" id="eq-a"><span class="t-math" data-display>x = 1</span><span class="t-eqno">(1)</span></div>
<p>See <a class="t-ref" href="#eq-a">(1)</a>.</p>
````

## math/class-only-not-numbered

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math elements#numbering

A class on the closer does not number the equation; only an id does. `div.t-eq` has the classes `t-eq`, then the author's classes.

````tern
$$
x = 1
$$ {.wide}
````

````html
<div class="t-eq wide"><span class="t-math" data-display>x = 1</span></div>
````

## math/content-not-tern

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math syntax-blocks#r4

The body is verbatim TeX: emphasis, references, links and code spans are not recognised (so `@eq-a` is no reference and gets no `ref.dangling`).

````tern
$$
*a* + @eq-a + [b](c) + `d`
$$
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>*a* + @eq-a + [b](c) + `d`</span></div>
````

## math/content-escaped

spec: syntax-blocks#display-math syntax-blocks#r4

The TeX is HTML-escaped and otherwise unchanged: `<` and `&` are escaped, and an entity is not passed through.

````tern
$$
a < b & c \\ d &amp; e
$$
````

````html
<div class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display>a &lt; b &amp; c \\ d &amp;amp; e</span></div>
````
