# Autolinks

Cases for autolinks (angle and bare forms) and the `http(s)://` row of the ambiguity rules.

## autolink/angle-url

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

CommonMark's angle autolink; `&` is escaped in both the `href` and the text.

````tern
<https://a.b/c?d=1&e=2>
````

````html
<p><a href="https://a.b/c?d=1&amp;e=2">https://a.b/c?d=1&amp;e=2</a></p>
````

## autolink/angle-email

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

An angle e-mail autolink gets `mailto:`.

````tern
<me@x.org>
````

````html
<p><a href="mailto:me@x.org">me@x.org</a></p>
````

## autolink/angle-schemes

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

Any scheme works in the angle form, `mailto:` included.

````tern
<ftp://x.org/f> and <mailto:me@x.org>
````

````html
<p><a href="ftp://x.org/f">ftp://x.org/f</a> and <a href="mailto:me@x.org">mailto:me@x.org</a></p>
````

## autolink/angle-space-text

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#algorithm

A space ends any chance of an angle autolink, and `<` is not in the bare form's start set: all text.

````tern
<https://a b>
````

````html
<p>&lt;https://a b&gt;</p>
````

## autolink/angle-atom

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#algorithm

An angle autolink is an atom: the stars inside are not emphasis.

````tern
<https://x.org/*a*>
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.org/*a*">https://x.org/*a*</a></p>
````

## autolink/angle-glued-group

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-blocks#positions

A strong group glued after an autolink attaches to it.

````tern
<https://x.org>{.ext}
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.org" class="ext">https://x.org</a></p>
````

## autolink/bare-spelling

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

The bare form: the final `.` is trimmed.

````tern
See https://x.com/_a_/docs.
````

````html
<p>See <a href="https://x.com/_a_/docs">https://x.com/_a_/docs</a>.</p>
````

## autolink/bare-after-paren-and-bracket

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

A bare URL may start after `(` or `[`; the unbalanced `)` is trimmed and the `]` stops it.

````tern
(https://a.b) and [https://a.b]
````

````html
<p>(<a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a>) and [<a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a>]</p>
````

## autolink/bare-after-quotes

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

A bare URL may start after `"` or `'`; `"` stops it and a trailing `'` is trimmed.

````tern
"https://a.b" and 'https://a.b'
````

````html
<p>"<a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a>" and '<a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a>'</p>
````

## autolink/bare-in-emphasis

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#emphasis

A bare URL may start after `*`, and a trailing `*` is trimmed, so the emphasis closes around it.

````tern
*https://a.b*
````

````html
<p><em><a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a></em></p>
````

## autolink/bare-after-letter-text

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#ambiguity

After a letter, a digit or any character outside the start set, `https://` is text.

````tern
xhttps://a.b, 1https://a.b and a/https://a.b
````

````html
<p>xhttps://a.b, 1https://a.b and a/https://a.b</p>
````

## autolink/bare-stops-at-angle

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

`<` and `>` stop a bare URL.

````tern
https://a.b/c<d

https://a.b/c>d
````

````html
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>&lt;d</p>
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>&gt;d</p>
````

## autolink/bare-stops-at-brackets-braces

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-blocks#positions

`[` and `{` stop a bare URL; the weak `{f}` glued after it stays text.

````tern
https://a.b/c[d] https://a.b/e{f}
````

````html
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>[d] <a href="https://a.b/e">https://a.b/e</a>{f}</p>
````

## autolink/bare-stops-pipe-quote-backtick

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

`|`, `"` and a backtick stop a bare URL.

````tern
x https://a.b/c|d https://a.b/e"f" https://a.b/g`h`
````

````html
<p>x <a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>|d <a href="https://a.b/e">https://a.b/e</a>"f" <a href="https://a.b/g">https://a.b/g</a><code>h</code></p>
````

## autolink/bare-trim-more

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

Trailing `:`, `;` and `?` are trimmed too.

````tern
Try https://x.org/a: or https://x.org/b; maybe https://x.org/c?
````

````html
<p>Try <a href="https://x.org/a">https://x.org/a</a>: or <a href="https://x.org/b">https://x.org/b</a>; maybe <a href="https://x.org/c">https://x.org/c</a>?</p>
````

## autolink/bare-trim-repeats

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

Trimming repeats until stable (as in GFM): `).` loses the `.` and then the unbalanced `)`, and `**` loses both stars.

````tern
(see https://x.org).

**https://x.org**
````

````html
<p>(see <a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a>).</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a></strong></p>
````

## autolink/bare-wikipedia-parens

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

Balanced parentheses stay in the URL; only an unbalanced final `)` is trimmed.

````tern
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar)

(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar))
````

````html
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar)">https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar)</a></p>
<p>(<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar)">https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foo_(bar)</a>)</p>
````

## autolink/bare-atom-no-emphasis

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#algorithm

A bare URL is an atom: `*` and `~~` inside it are not delimiters.

````tern
https://x.org/a*b*c and https://x.org/~~d~~e
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.org/a*b*c">https://x.org/a*b*c</a> and <a href="https://x.org/~~d~~e">https://x.org/~~d~~e</a></p>
````

## autolink/bare-atom-no-element

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#algorithm syntax-inline#inline-element

The URL consumes `:kbd` and stops at `[`, so no element opens and `[x]` is text.

````tern
https://x.org/:kbd[x]
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.org/:kbd">https://x.org/:kbd</a>[x]</p>
````

## autolink/bare-glued-group

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-blocks#positions

`{` stops a bare URL, and a strong group glued there attaches to the link.

````tern
https://x.org{.ext}
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.org" class="ext">https://x.org</a></p>
````

## autolink/bare-table-cell

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-blocks#tables

A bare URL never consumes `|`, so the row keeps its cells.

````tern
| URL | Note |
|---|---|
|https://a.b|note|
````

````html
<table><thead><tr><th>URL</th><th>Note</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><a href="https://a.b">https://a.b</a></td><td>note</td></tr></tbody></table>
````

## autolink/bare-literal-escape

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#ambiguity syntax-inline#escape

`https\://` is the literal spelling.

````tern
https\://x.org
````

````html
<p>https://x.org</p>
````

## autolink/bare-in-code-and-math

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#ambiguity

Inside code or math a URL is not a link.

````tern
`https://x.org` and $https://x.org$
````

````html
<p><code>https://x.org</code> and <span class="t-math">https://x.org</span></p>
````

## autolink/bare-ampersand

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#soft-break

`&` in a bare URL is escaped in both the `href` and the text.

````tern
https://a.b/?x=1&y=2
````

````html
<p><a href="https://a.b/?x=1&amp;y=2">https://a.b/?x=1&amp;y=2</a></p>
````

## autolink/bare-trims-mark-closer

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#strike-highlight

`~` and `=` are in the bare-URL trim set, so the URL gives back a closing `==` or `~~` and a highlighted or struck-through URL is a link inside `<mark>` or `<del>`.

````tern
==https://x.org==

~~https://x.org~~
````

````html
<p><mark><a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a></mark></p>
<p><del><a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a></del></p>
````

## autolink/bare-trims-backslash

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#escape

`\` is in the bare-URL trim set, so a URL at the end of a line does not swallow a hard break's `\`.

````tern
See https://x.org\
next line
````

````html
<p>See <a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a><br>
next line</p>
````

## autolink/bare-after-raw-tag

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#raw-inline-html

`>` is in the bare-URL start set, so a URL right after an inline raw tag links; `<` stops it before the end tag.

````tern
<b>https://x.org</b>
````

````html
<p><b><a href="https://x.org">https://x.org</a></b></p>
````

## autolink/bare-scheme-case-insensitive

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks

The scheme is matched case-insensitively; the URL is used as written.

````tern
HTTPS://x.org and Http://y.org
````

````html
<p><a href="HTTPS://x.org">HTTPS://x.org</a> and <a href="Http://y.org">Http://y.org</a></p>
````

## autolink/bare-scheme-alone-text

spec: syntax-inline#autolinks syntax-inline#ambiguity

A scheme with nothing after it is not a link: `http://` alone, or followed only by characters that trimming removes, is text.

````tern
Type http:// or https://. first.
````

````html
<p>Type http:// or https://. first.</p>
````
