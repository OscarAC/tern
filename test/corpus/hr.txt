# Thematic breaks and front matter

`---` is the documented rule; `***`, `___` and spaced forms are accepted
alternates. Front matter is not supported and says so.

## hr/dash

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break

`---` alone on a line is a rule.

````tern
---
````

````html
<hr>
````

## hr/star-and-underscore

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#r8

`***` and `___` are accepted, undocumented spellings of the rule.

````tern
***
___
````

````html
<hr>
<hr>
````

## hr/spaced

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#r8 syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Spaced forms are rules, and rules are tested before list items, so `- - -` and `* * *` are not nested lists.

````tern
- - -
* * *
_ _ _
````

````html
<hr>
<hr>
<hr>
````

## hr/long-runs

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break

Three or more of the same character make a rule.

````tern
----------
*****
_____
````

````html
<hr>
<hr>
<hr>
````

## hr/rule-on-remainder

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#blockquote

The rule test applies to the whole remainder: `- ---` is dashes and spaces only, so it is a rule (not a list item); `> ---` is a quote holding a rule.

````tern
- ---

> ---
````

````html
<hr>
<blockquote><hr></blockquote>
````

## hr/two-is-text

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break

Two characters are not a rule.

````tern
--
**
__
````

````html
<p>--
**
__</p>
````

## hr/mixed-is-text

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-inline#ambiguity

The characters must all be the same, and nothing else may be on the line.

````tern
-_-
_-_
-- x
````

````html
<p>-_-
_-_
-- x</p>
````

## hr/trailing-text-not-rule

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

A rule-like start followed by text is a paragraph, not a rule and not a list item.

````tern
--- x
````

````html
<p>--- x</p>
````

## hr/not-emphasis

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-inline#emphasis

`***both***` is not a rule (it holds letters); it is strong emphasis inside emphasis.

````tern
***both***
````

````html
<p><em><strong>both</strong></em></p>
````

## hr/indented

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#r2
options: escaped

Leading whitespace is ignored: an indented rule is still a rule.

````tern
      ---
\t***
````

````html
<hr>
<hr>
````

## hr/interrupts-paragraph

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Row 6: a rule interrupts a paragraph (and makes no setext heading).

````tern
Some prose
---
````

````html
<p>Some prose</p>
<hr>
````

## hr/attrs-not-accepted

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#positions

A thematic break takes no attributes: with a group after it the line is no longer a rule, and the group is ordinary text.

````tern
--- {.thin}
````

````html
<p>--- {.thin}</p>
````

## hr/attribute-line-before-rule

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break syntax-blocks#attribute-line

An attribute line before `---` attaches to the `<hr>`: only a trailing group is refused on a rule.

````tern
{.thin}
---
````

````html
<hr class="thin">
````

````diagnostics
````

## hr/tabs-between-marks

spec: syntax-blocks#thematic-break
options: escaped

Rule lines allow tabs as well as spaces between the marks.

````tern
-\t-\t-
*\t* *
_ \t_\t_
````

````html
<hr>
<hr>
<hr>
````

## hr/frontmatter-info

spec: syntax-blocks#front-matter elements#meta-rules syntax-blocks#r1

A note whose first block is `---`, `key: value` lines and `---` renders as a rule, a paragraph and a rule, with an info pointing to `:::meta`.

````tern
---
title: Linear maps
lang: en
---
````

````html
<hr>
<p>title: Linear maps
lang: en</p>
<hr>
````

````diagnostics
1:1 info doc.frontmatter ":::meta"
````

## hr/frontmatter-not-first-block

spec: syntax-blocks#front-matter

The same lines after another block are just a rule, a paragraph and a rule.

````tern
# Notes

---
title: x
---
````

````html
<h1 id="notes">Notes</h1>
<hr>
<p>title: x</p>
<hr>
````

## hr/frontmatter-needs-key-value

spec: syntax-blocks#front-matter

Prose between two rules at the start of a note is not front matter.

````tern
---
Just a paragraph between rules.
---
````

````html
<hr>
<p>Just a paragraph between rules.</p>
<hr>
````

## hr/frontmatter-after-blank-line

spec: syntax-blocks#front-matter diagnostics#doc-frontmatter

A note usually starts with a blank line after the tern.js line; front matter is detected on the first block wherever it starts, and `doc.frontmatter` points at the opening `---` line, here line 2.

````tern

---
title: x
---
````

````html
<hr>
<p>title: x</p>
<hr>
````

````diagnostics
2:1 info doc.frontmatter
````
