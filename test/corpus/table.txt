# Tables

Pipe tables and the `:::table[Caption]` merge. Diagnostics
positioned at "the line" use the column where the line's remainder starts (column 1
outside quotes and list items); `table.extra-cell` sits at the `|` before the row's
first extra cell, once per row.

## table/spec-example

spec: syntax-blocks#tables syntax-blocks#attribute-line

The tables example: an attribute line, alignment, an escaped pipe and a continued row.

````tern
{.wide}
| Phase | Weeks | Note |
|---|--:|---|
| P2 | 2 | golden DOM \| diff |
| P3 | 3 | smoke green;\
  no task over 50 ms |
````

````html
<table class="wide">
<thead><tr><th>Phase</th><th style="text-align:right">Weeks</th><th>Note</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>P2</td><td style="text-align:right">2</td><td>golden DOM | diff</td></tr>
<tr><td>P3</td><td style="text-align:right">3</td><td>smoke green;<br>
no task over 50 ms</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
````

## table/basic

spec: syntax-blocks#tables syntax-blocks#r1

A `|` line followed by a delimiter row starts a table; the first line is the header.

````tern
| a | b |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
````

````html
<table>
<thead><tr><th>a</th><th>b</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr></tbody>
</table>
````

````json ast
{"type": "root", "children": [
  {"type": "table", "align": [null, null], "children": [
    {"type": "tableRow", "children": [
      {"type": "tableCell", "children": [{"type": "text", "value": "a"}]},
      {"type": "tableCell", "children": [{"type": "text", "value": "b"}]}
    ]},
    {"type": "tableRow", "children": [
      {"type": "tableCell", "children": [{"type": "text", "value": "1"}]},
      {"type": "tableCell", "children": [{"type": "text", "value": "2"}]}
    ]}
  ]}
]}
````
