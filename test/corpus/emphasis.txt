# Emphasis, strong, strikethrough and highlight

Cases for emphasis, strong, strikethrough and highlight, and their rows of the ambiguity rules. CommonMark's numbered `*`
examples are imported elsewhere; these are Tern's own.

## emphasis/spelling

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

The three spellings of emphasis and strong.

````tern
*em*, **strong**, ***both***
````

````html
<p><em>em</em>, <strong>strong</strong>, <em><strong>both</strong></em></p>
````

## emphasis/spaced-stars-text

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#ambiguity

A `*` with whitespace on both sides is neither left- nor right-flanking; a later closer then has no opener.

````tern
2 * 3 * 4 and x * a*
````

````html
<p>2 * 3 * 4 and x * a*</p>
````

## emphasis/intraword

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

`*` may open and close inside a word, as on GitHub.

````tern
x*y*z
````

````html
<p>x<em>y</em>z</p>
````

## emphasis/multiple-of-three-skip

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

The `**` can both open and close, and 1 + 2 is a multiple of 3, so it does not pair with either `*`; the outer pair matches and the `**` is text.

````tern
*a**b*
````

````html
<p><em>a**b</em></p>
````

## emphasis/multiple-of-three-nest

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

The rule of 3 keeps the first `**` from closing the `*`, so the two `**` pair inside the emphasis.

````tern
*x**y**z*
````

````html
<p><em>x<strong>y</strong>z</em></p>
````

## emphasis/partial-run

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

A closer uses only as many stars as it has; the opener's leftover star is text.

````tern
**a*
````

````html
<p>*<em>a</em></p>
````

## emphasis/punctuation-flanking

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

A `*` after a letter and before punctuation is not left-flanking, so `a*"b"*` has no opener; a `*` at the start before punctuation is.

````tern
a*"b"* is text.

*(a)* is emphasis.
````

````html
<p>a*"b"* is text.</p>
<p><em>(a)</em> is emphasis.</p>
````

## emphasis/unmatched-text

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#ambiguity

An opener with no closer is text.

````tern
*a and b
````

````html
<p>*a and b</p>
````

## emphasis/underscore-pairs-text

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#ambiguity

Underscore runs of any length are text, even where `*` would be emphasis.

````tern
_a_ and __b__ and ___c___
````

````html
<p>_a_ and __b__ and ___c___</p>
````

## emphasis/underscore-in-url

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#autolinks

`_a_` inside a bare URL is neither emphasis nor a stop.

````tern
https://x.com/_a_/b
````

````html
<p><a href="https://x.com/_a_/b">https://x.com/_a_/b</a></p>
````

## emphasis/underscore-inside-em

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis

An underscore inside emphasis is just a character.

````tern
*snake_case*
````

````html
<p><em>snake_case</em></p>
````

## emphasis/glued-strong-group

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions

A strong group glued to a `**` closer attaches to the `<strong>`.

````tern
**b**{.y}
````

````html
<p><strong class="y">b</strong></p>
````

## emphasis/glued-group-triple-star

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions

`***x***` is `<em><strong>x</strong></em>`, and a strong group glued to its closer binds to the outer element, the `<em>`.

````tern
***x***{.c}
````

````html
<p><em class="c"><strong>x</strong></em></p>
````

## emphasis/glued-id-on-em

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions elements#ids

An `#id` glued to a `*` closer goes on the `<em>`.

````tern
*a*{#e}
````

````html
<p><em id="e">a</em></p>
````

## emphasis/glued-weak-group-text

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions

After a delimiter closer only strong groups attach; `{open}` stays text, silently.

````tern
**b**{open}
````

````html
<p><strong>b</strong>{open}</p>
````

## emphasis/glued-malformed-hash

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions diagnostics#attr-malformed

A candidate starting with `#` that does not parse stays text, with `attr.malformed` (W) at the first bad column.

````tern
**b**{#x "y"}
````

````html
<p><strong>b</strong>{#x "y"}</p>
````

````diagnostics
1:10 warning attr.malformed
````

## emphasis/glued-malformed-key-silent

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions

After a delimiter closer, a bad candidate that does not start with `#` or `.` is text with no diagnostic.

````tern
**b**{x "y"}
````

````html
<p><strong>b</strong>{x "y"}</p>
````

## emphasis/spaced-group-text

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-blocks#positions

A group after a space is not glued.

````tern
**b** {.y}
````

````html
<p><strong>b</strong> {.y}</p>
````

## emphasis/link-inside

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#links

Emphasis may contain a link.

````tern
*[a](u)*
````

````html
<p><em><a href="u">a</a></em></p>
````

## emphasis/cannot-cross-link

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#algorithm

Delimiters inside link text are resolved there: the inner `*` cannot close the outer opener.

````tern
*[a*](u)
````

````html
<p>*<a href="u">a*</a></p>
````

## emphasis/cannot-cross-element

spec: syntax-inline#emphasis syntax-inline#algorithm syntax-inline#inline-element

The same holds for inline-element content: emphasis cannot cross the element's brackets.

````tern
*a :kbd[b* c]
````

````html
<p>*a <kbd class="kbd" data-t="kbd">b* c</kbd></p>
````

## emphasis/del-and-mark

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight

The two spellings of strikethrough and highlight.

````tern
~~gone~~ and ==key==
````

````html
<p><del>gone</del> and <mark>key</mark></p>
````

## emphasis/strike-glued-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#ambiguity

`~~` glued to ASCII letters on the outside can neither open nor close.

````tern
x~~y~~z
````

````html
<p>x~~y~~z</p>
````

## emphasis/tilde-path-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#ambiguity

A single `~` is never a delimiter.

````tern
~/.vimrc and ~user and a~b
````

````html
<p>~/.vimrc and ~user and a~b</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-in-parens

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight

Punctuation outside is not an ASCII letter or digit, so `(==x==)` highlights.

````tern
(==x==)
````

````html
<p>(<mark>x</mark>)</p>
````

## emphasis/cjk-glue

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-blocks#character-classes

The glue test is ASCII only, so `==` and `~~` work between CJK characters.

````tern
これは==重要==です

中文~~删除~~中文
````

````html
<p>これは<mark>重要</mark>です</p>
<p>中文<del>删除</del>中文</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-non-ascii-letter

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-blocks#character-classes

A consequence of the ASCII test: non-ASCII letters outside the run do not block it.

````tern
α==β==γ
````

````html
<p>α<mark>β</mark>γ</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-after-underscore

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-blocks#character-classes

`_` is not an ASCII letter or digit, so it does not block a run.

````tern
_==x==_
````

````html
<p>_<mark>x</mark>_</p>
````

## emphasis/run-lengths-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#ambiguity

Runs of one, or of three or more, are text.

````tern
a ~x~ b ===y=== c ~~~z~~~ d ====w====
````

````html
<p>a ~x~ b ===y=== c ~~~z~~~ d ====w====</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-glued-open-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight

The opening `==` follows an ASCII letter, so it cannot open; the closing one has nothing to close.

````tern
a==b==
````

````html
<p>a==b==</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-glued-close-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight

The closing `==` is followed by an ASCII letter, so it cannot close.

````tern
==b==c
````

````html
<p>==b==c</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-flanking-space-text

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight

`==` uses `*`'s flanking: an opener followed by a space, or a closer preceded by one, does not count.

````tern
== x ==
````

````html
<p>== x ==</p>
````

## emphasis/del-around-strong

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#emphasis

`~~` and `**` nest through the one stack.

````tern
~~**a**~~
````

````html
<p><del><strong>a</strong></del></p>
````

## emphasis/shared-stack

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-inline#algorithm

`~~` and `==` resolve through the same stack: when the `~~` pair closes, the `==` opener between them is dropped, and the last `==` has nothing to close.

````tern
~~a ==b~~ c==
````

````html
<p><del>a ==b</del> c==</p>
````

## emphasis/mark-del-glued-groups

spec: syntax-inline#strike-highlight syntax-blocks#positions

Strong groups glued to `==` and `~~` closers attach to `<mark>` and `<del>`.

````tern
==k=={.x} and ~~g~~{#d}
````

````html
<p><mark class="x">k</mark> and <del id="d">g</del></p>
````
