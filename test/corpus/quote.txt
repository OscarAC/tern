# Blockquotes (and the quote prefix of the line algorithm)

`> ` or a bare `>` opens a quote; `>5` does not. The quote is a prefix
container: lines continue it by starting with `>`, or lazily.

## quote/needs-space

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-inline#ambiguity

A quote needs a space, a tab or the end of the line after `>`: `>5 people` and `>= 3` are paragraphs.

````tern
>5 people came
>= 3 is enough
````

````html
<p>&gt;5 people came
&gt;= 3 is enough</p>
````

## quote/lazy-continuation

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

The blockquote example: a paragraph line without `>` continues the quoted paragraph lazily.

````tern
> A quote,
lazily continued.
````

````html
<blockquote><p>A quote,
lazily continued.</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/bare-marker-continues

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote

A bare `>` line is a blank line inside the quote: it ends the paragraph but not the quote.

````tern
> First paragraph.
>
> Second paragraph.
````

````html
<blockquote><p>First paragraph.</p><p>Second paragraph.</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/blank-line-ends

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote

A blank line ends the quote, so two quotes separated by a blank line stay two quotes.

````tern
> First quote.

> Second quote.

Not quoted.
````

````html
<blockquote><p>First quote.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>Second quote.</p></blockquote>
<p>Not quoted.</p>
````

## quote/tab-after-marker

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm
options: escaped

A tab after `>` opens a quote like a space.

````tern
>\tTabbed
````

````html
<blockquote><p>Tabbed</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/empty

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

`>` alone on a line opens an empty quote.

````tern
>
````

````html
<blockquote></blockquote>
````

## quote/one-space-stripped

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#lists

Exactly one space after `>` is stripped: `>   b` leaves `b` at the item's content column (2), `>  c` leaves `c` at column 1, outside the item.

````tern
> - a
>
>   b
>
>  c
````

````html
<blockquote><ul><li><p>a</p><p>b</p></li></ul><p>c</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/nests-container

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote

The rest of a quoted line is parsed as blocks, so `> :::note` nests a container.

````tern
> :::note
> Inside.
> :::
````

````html
<blockquote><div class="t-block note" data-t="note"><div class="t-title"><span class="t-label">Note</span></div><p>Inside.</p></div></blockquote>
````

## quote/nests-list

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#lists

`> - item` nests a list.

````tern
> - item
> - item two
````

````html
<blockquote><ul><li>item</li><li>item two</li></ul></blockquote>
````

## quote/nested-quotes

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote

`> > ` nests a quote in a quote.

````tern
> > deep
````

````html
<blockquote><blockquote><p>deep</p></blockquote></blockquote>
````

## quote/glued-double-marker-is-text

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-inline#ambiguity

`>>` is not two quote markers: the first `>` is followed by `>`, not by a space, so the line is a paragraph (unlike CommonMark).

````tern
>> deep
````

````html
<p>&gt;&gt; deep</p>
````

## quote/continuation-without-space

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

The continuation prefix is `>` plus an *optional* space or tab, so `>b` inside an open quote continues it, although `>b` cannot open one.

````tern
> a
>b
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a
b</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/indented-marker

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#r2

Leading whitespace is ignored when a line is classified, so an indented `>` opens a quote (there is no indented code).

````tern
      > a
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/indented-continuation-marker

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#r2

Leading whitespace may precede the `>` of the quote prefix, so an indented `>` on a continuation line continues the quote. (Without that rule the prefix would fail, `> b` would interrupt, and this would be two quotes.)

````tern
> a
   > b
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a
b</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/interrupts-paragraph

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Row 8: a quote interrupts a paragraph.

````tern
Some prose
> A quote
````

````html
<p>Some prose</p>
<blockquote><p>A quote</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/no-lazy-after-heading

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

Lazy continuation needs an open paragraph; after a quoted heading the next line without `>` ends the quote.

````tern
> # Title
continued
````

````html
<blockquote><h1 id="title">Title</h1></blockquote>
<p>continued</p>
````

## quote/thematic-break-ends

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

A line that would interrupt a paragraph is not lazy, so `---` ends the quote (and is no setext underline).

````tern
> a
---
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a</p></blockquote>
<hr>
````

## quote/lazy-partial-prefix

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

When an inner prefix fails but an outer one matches, the line continues the innermost paragraph lazily.

````tern
> > a
> b
````

````html
<blockquote><blockquote><p>a
b</p></blockquote></blockquote>
````

## quote/lazy-in-list

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#lists

Laziness reaches through every failed prefix: `b` continues the paragraph of the item inside the quote.

````tern
> - a
b
````

````html
<blockquote><ul><li>a
b</li></ul></blockquote>
````

## quote/lazy-ordered-non-1

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#lists

The quote prefix fails and `2. b` starts a block, so it is not lazy: the quote closes and an ordered list starting at 2 begins (the lazy-continuation rule; as cmark).

````tern
> a
2. b
````

````html
<blockquote><p>a</p></blockquote>
<ol start="2"><li>b</li></ol>
````

## quote/ended-by-closer

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#closer
options: schema=none

A closer is neither `>` nor lazy (it interrupts), so it ends the quote and then closes the container.

````tern
:::box
> a
:::
````

````html
<div class="t-block box" data-t="box"><blockquote><p>a</p></blockquote></div>
````

## quote/tab-after-marker-list-column

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm syntax-blocks#columns syntax-blocks#lists
options: escaped

A tab after `>` is consumed whole by the prefix (Tern's simpler rule, unlike CommonMark's partial tab, which would keep two columns and put `b` inside the item), so `b` starts at column 0 of the remainder, outside the item.

````tern
> - a
>
>\tb
````

````html
<blockquote><ul><li>a</li></ul><p>b</p></blockquote>
````

## quote/nesting-limit

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm

The 65th nested quote opener would make the stack deeper than 64, so from there the line is paragraph text, with `nesting.limit` at that `>`. The root is not counted, so 64 nested quotes are allowed.

````tern js
'> '.repeat(65) + 'x\n'
````

````diagnostics
1:129 error nesting.limit
````

## quote/depth-shared-with-containers

spec: syntax-blocks#blockquote syntax-blocks#line-algorithm
options: schema=none

Containers, list items and quotes share one depth: after 63 containers, the first quote is level 64 and the second is refused.

````tern js
':::d\n'.repeat(63) + '> > x\n' + ':::\n'.repeat(63)
````

````diagnostics
64:3 error nesting.limit
````
